About Me

Daniel Abisrur is an educator and content designer who has coached TED speakers, guided tours, and served as lead writer for Questomania’s mobile escape rooms.

With a degree in Literature and Education (graduated with honors), he specializes in adventure-based learning and even created an escape room inspired by Orwell’s 1984.

His work has since expanded to include unique pub quizzes and creative writing workshops that transform ideas into vibrant stories.
