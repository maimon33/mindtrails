A roller-coaster trivia ride. Questions that will make you laugh, surprise you, and make you reach for all the unique knowledge you have collected throughout your life.

The best part isn’t knowing the answer. It’s what happens before it.

60-90 min · Up to 60 peopleSee what makes it different

  • A. Multiple-choice questions
  • B. Serious IQ tests
  • C. Random guessing games
  • D. Of course, all of the above

This is trivia where knowing things helps, but thinking together matters more. Questions twist familiar formats, make people look at information differently and give the team something to discuss before anyone can shout out an answer.

For storytelling and communication

Into My Mind

A real storytelling workshop

Before AI, stories were already changing the world. This is a hands-on workshop about how to build, shape and tell stories that people actually remember.

People rarely remember information. They remember stories.

60-90 min · Flexible group sizeExplore the workshop

Before AI, stories were already changing the world

Storytelling is not a soft skill. It is one of the oldest tools humans have for making another person listen, care, understand and remember. This workshop turns that power into something participants can actually use.

What will we explore?

  • What makes a story emotionally memorable
  • Why do people trust stories more than information
  • What are the three core ingredients behind an engaging story
  • How do you turn information into something another person actually wants to hear

What makes it a real workshop?

Participants do not just hear storytelling principles. They use them. They work with ideas, build stories, test what makes them engaging and practice turning information into something another person can follow and remember. Not a lecture about storytelling. We tell stories.

Best forCommunication · Leadership · Presentations · Employer branding · Teams working with ideas

For better team understanding

Share My Mind

A workshop about people, connection, and too much pizza

Communication styles. Misunderstandings. Decisions. The strange fact that two people can experience exactly the same situation completely differently.

Communication is not what you say. It’s what the other person experiences.

60-90 min · Up to 12 peopleSee how it works

In a room with three people, there are already four different realities

Share My Mind is a creative group activity about personality, decision-making and the people beside us. Instead of explaining communication through slides and models, the group gets to experience what happens when different people interpret the same situation in completely different ways.

What will we explore?

  • Communication styles
  • Collaboration under uncertainty
  • Misunderstandings
  • Identity inside a team
  • And possibly the most important workplace issue of all, too much pizza

What does it bring to the team?

A chance to notice how differently people think, communicate and react, without turning the session into therapy or another corporate communication lecture.

Best forSmall teams · Communication · New teams · Onboarding · Team reflection

For creativity and fresh thinking

Out of My Mind

Creative thinking, out loud

Can a team stay creative when everyone is afraid of getting it wrong? Out of My Mind is an active and humorous workshop about thinking patterns, curiosity, play and the occasionally strange choices our brains make.

The team discovers the unique perspective each person brings, and how to turn those differences into an advantage that strengthens collaboration.

60-90 min · Up to 20 peopleStep outside the usual

What happens in the room

Through playful challenges, participants discover how changing perspective can unlock new ways to approach a problem.

What will we explore?

  • Why humans need to play
  • The critical mind versus the creative mind
  • How different ways of thinking improve both the way we work and the quality of our results
  • Thinking traps, creative choices and motivation through play

Best forInnovation days · Creativity · Team energy · Wellbeing · Teams that need a reset

Quick answers

The questions we get first

Do we need a special room?

No. Any room where people can move around is enough. We have run events in canteens and open-plan offices.

How many people can take part?

From 4 to 40 in a single session. For larger groups we run sessions in parallel.

Can you run it in English?

Yes. The experiences are built for international teams and run in English.

Minimal. We arrive before your team, set up, and take everything away afterwards.
